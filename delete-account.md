# Delete your Kite account

## In the app (the fastest way)

1. Open Kite and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

This deletes your budget and your sign-in record from Kite's database, and, if
you signed in with Apple, ends the Sign in with Apple link for Kite. It also
clears the phone. It cannot be undone.

## If you no longer have the app

Email **kakodjenou@gmail.com** from the address on your Kite account (for an
Apple account that hides your email, tell me the relay address shown in the
app's Account screen, or the Apple ID you used). Say you want your account
deleted. I will delete it and reply once it is done, normally within a few days.

## What is deleted, and what is kept

- Deleted: your budget, your sign-in ID and email, and, for Apple accounts, the
  stored sign-in token.
- Routine database backups kept by the hosting provider can hold your data for a
  short while after deletion, days not months, before they expire.
- Nothing is kept for analytics, because Kite has none.

See the [privacy policy](../privacy/) for everything Kite stores.
